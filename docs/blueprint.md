# **App Name**: The Evolve Room

## Core Features:

- Service & Course Directory: Browse a curated list of healing modalities, coaching services, and self-paced courses with brief descriptions.
- Detailed Offer Pages: Dedicated pages for each service and course, providing in-depth content, duration, and investment details.
- AI-Powered Modality Advisor: An AI tool that suggests relevant healing modalities or courses based on user-provided challenges or goals through a simple questionnaire.
- AI-Assisted Graphology Insights: An AI tool offering preliminary, indicative insights from an uploaded handwriting sample to pique interest in the Graphology service.
- Booking & Payment Gateway: A streamlined process to book 1:1 sessions, join LET Practice, or enroll in courses, integrating with payment systems like Razorpay and Stripe.
- Automated Confirmations & Access: Post-payment, users receive automated confirmation emails, Calendly booking links, and direct access information for courses or Zoom sessions.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary Color (for key elements, CTAs, headings): Champagne Gold (#E0C98F), providing a luxurious and empowering highlight against dark tones.
- Background Color: A deep, warm Charcoal Black (#161513) to establish a sophisticated, minimal, and calming canvas.
- Accent Color (for secondary details, subtle emphasis): Deep Beige (#998E7A), adding a soft, complementary warmth and depth.
- Headline and body font: 'PT Sans' (humanist sans-serif) for its modern, clean lines, and versatile readability across all content, reinforcing a minimal yet warm aesthetic.
- Clean, elegant line-art icons used sparingly to maintain the minimalist and luxurious feel, with a subtle feminine touch.
- Generous application of whitespace around content blocks and sections to create a sense of spaciousness, clarity, and premium presentation.
- Subtle, fluid micro-animations for hover states and transitions, enhancing user interaction without distracting from the serene and luxurious experience.
